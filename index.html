About

My work combines field biology, systematics, phylogenetic inference and high-throughput sequencing to investigate diversification, adaptation and biogeographic history.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at São Paulo State University (UNESP), working in evolutionary biology, systematics and phylogenomics. My research focuses primarily on Triatominae, including vectors of Chagas disease, and Theraphosidae, especially Neotropical tarantulas. I combine morphological analyses, genomic data, geometric morphometrics and comparative approaches to investigate species boundaries, evolutionary relationships and genomic variation.
